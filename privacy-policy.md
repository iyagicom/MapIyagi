# Privacy Policy for MapIyagi

**Last updated: October 21, 2026**

## Overview
MapIyagi is a map tool for viewing land parcels.

## Data Collection
MapIyagi does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the developer. Nothing about you is stored by the developer. It has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

## Network Use
To show maps, the app requests map data from the third-party map service it uses (V-World); that service receives the requests needed to display the map under its own policy.
